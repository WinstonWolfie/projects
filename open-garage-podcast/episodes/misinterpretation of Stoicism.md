Rough outline:

- "broicism"
  - ignores virtue
  - just focuses on being unemotional
  - misogynic
- similar to meditation/Buddhism's spread in the west
  - greatly simplified/secularised
  - touted as a panacea, solving multiple problems
  - the core is ignored
    - in Stoicism virtue is often less mentioned in modern authors
    - in meditations the original goal of enlightenment is watered down
